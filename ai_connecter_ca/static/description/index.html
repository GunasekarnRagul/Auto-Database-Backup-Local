03 / Storage

Smart Storage Retention

Automated cleanup keeps your Nextcloud lean. You set the rules — it handles the rest.

Auto Cleanup

Self-hosted storage isn't unlimited. Our built-in retention algorithm automatically scans your Nextcloud folder and purges outdated backups based on count or age thresholds you define.

01
Count-based or age-based cleanup
Keep only the last $X$ backups, or retain backups for $X$ days — or disable cleanup for permanent archiving.
02
Separate rules for Auto vs. Manual
Configure independent retention policies for scheduled and on-demand backups.
03
Prevents storage quota errors
No surprise "storage full" blocks — only keep exactly what you need.

We don't just backup text. The module exports your entire database into a compressed .zip, with an optional toggle to include your Odoo filestore (images, documents, attachments). Supports all databases or selective sync.

01
Full database .zip exports

03
Timestamped dynamic folder structure
Backups organized into timestamped folders (e.g. Auto_12-03-2026_14-30-00) for easy navigation.
